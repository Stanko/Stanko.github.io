After almost two years (life and other projects happened), I finally plotted something new. I resurrected the Moon project, cleaned it up and published it online. Using a technical pen allowed me to add more detail to the image. It reminded me why I love pen plotting so much, and I hope to plot more in the near future.

The code is available on my [GitHub](https://github.com/Stanko/moon), and you can generate your own Moon [here](https://muffinman.io/moon/).
