I participated in my first pixel art jam! It was a [Game Boy themed one](https://muffinman-io.itch.io/gb-pixel-art-jam-2026-entry-spacedeck-trio). It was a lot of fun to work with such a limited palette. The theme was **Three**, and I went with three player ships from SpaceDeck X. I was happy to see how well my sprites translate into the Game Boy's original colors.

Once I finished drawing it, I had to animate it, so here is the animated version:

<Image
  src="./img/trio.gif"
  alt="Pixel art animation of three player ships from my game SpaceDeck X facing a boss. Four colors, Game Boy palette."
  size="wide"
/>

I even toyed with the idea of making a small demo game for it (not a real Game Boy cartridge), but due to the time limit, I went with a single animation (which has 48 frames).

I use Aseprite to draw and animate. You can find my jam submission [here](https://itch.io/jam/gb-pixel-art-jam-2026). Hope you like it!
